Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026

autoSocial Poster (“the app”) is a personal tool that publishes a shop owner’s own product videos and photos to their own connected social accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and what it does not.

1 Who runs the app

The app is operated by an individual developer (“we”) for use by a small number of retail shop owners. It is not a public service and has no user accounts of its own. Each operator connects their own Google account and their own social media accounts.

2 What the app accesses on TikTok

When you authorize the app for your TikTok account, it requests only:

  • video.upload and video.publish — to upload and post the video or photo files that you have placed in your own folder.
  • Creator info (your username, nickname, avatar URL, and available privacy settings) — read once before each post because the TikTok Content Posting API requires it to show you correct posting options.

The app never reads your TikTok messages, follower lists, analytics, or any content it did not post.

3 What data is stored, and where

The app stores its working data in the operator’s own Google Sheets and Google Drive. We do not run a database or a server that holds your data.

  • Media files — the videos and photos to be posted, kept in the operator’s Google Drive.
  • A posting log — caption text, hashtags, post status, and the resulting post link, kept in the operator’s Google Sheet.
  • Access tokens — the tokens that let the app post on your behalf, kept in the app’s private script settings and never shown to anyone else.

4 Caption generation

To draft captions, the app may send the product description you wrote and a few of your past captions to Google’s Gemini API. It does not send your TikTok account details, tokens, or any personal information to that service.

5 What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use your data to build profiles. We do not post anything you did not provide.

6 Retention and deletion

Media files are moved into a dated archive folder after posting and are deleted by the operator when no longer needed. You can revoke the app’s access to your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok settings under Security & permissions → Manage app permissions; once revoked, the app can no longer post or read anything.

To have any stored caption log or token removed, email the address below and it will be deleted within 30 days.

7 Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 18.

8 Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated. Material changes will be communicated to current operators by email.
